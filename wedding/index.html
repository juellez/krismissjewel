We are so excited to celebrate our wedding weekend with you in the Ozarks! Formal invitations with specific venue details and event timing will follow in the mail. In the meantime, here is early travel information to help you plan your trip.

Eureka Springs is a historic mountain town filled with winding streets, Victorian architecture, and outdoor adventure. It's a fantastic weekend getaway, and we encourage guests with extra time to make a trip out of it! Check out Visit Eureka Springs for local dining and activity highlights.

Planning Ahead

Travel & Getting Around

Flying In (XNA)

Nearest Airport: Northwest Arkansas National Airport (XNA) is roughly a 1-hour scenic drive to Eureka Springs.

West Coast Travelers: Direct flights into XNA are limited, so expect a quick connection (commonly through Dallas, Denver, or Chicago). Renting a car at XNA is recommended for getting into town.

Amtrak & Regional Travel

Amtrak Travelers: The nearest Amtrak stations are in Kansas City, MO or Little Rock, AR. Commercial buses (like Jefferson Lines) connect from Kansas City down to Fayetteville, AR, where you can rent a car for the final 50-minute drive into the mountains.

Tulsa Connection: Regional buses also connect Tulsa, OK to Fayetteville for those traveling through Oklahoma.

Parking & Trolley System

No Car Needed Downtown: Eureka Springs has narrow streets and limited downtown parking, but you won't need to drive! The city operates a quaint open-air trolley system with loops connecting major hotel areas straight to downtown.

Wedding Shuttles: We will also arrange dedicated shuttle transport between primary hotel hubs and the ceremony/reception venues!

Lodging Options

Where to Stay

Eureka Springs is a popular weekend destination, and because the UFO Convention will be in town the same weekend, hotels may fill up quickly. We strongly recommend reserving early.

Recommended Hotels

  • Basin Park Hotel: Located in the heart of downtown where our reception will be held. Built in 1905, this is a convenient option for those who don't mind crooked floors and small spaces ... or the ocassional ghost.
  • Forest Springs Resort: A brand-new hotel nestled in the trees just off the "ridge" highway that circles town for a convenient stay. Has group spaces for socializing and live music.
  • The Wanderoo Lodge: A casual, queer friendly & popular local spot with economic room options, bar/restaurant onsite with live music, and outdoor firepit.

Cabins & RV Parks

  • Lake Leatherwood City Park: A beautiful park with RV hookups, primitive campsites, and cabins. Best of all, it sits on the Purple Trolley Route, so you can ride the trolley straight from your campsite into downtown!
  • Cottages & AirBnBs: There are countless historic cottages, AirBnB/VRBO cabins, and B&Bs tucked throughout the hills surrounding town. You can check out some managed by our friends at Blessed Bohmeian Properties.
